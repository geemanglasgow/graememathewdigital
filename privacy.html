Privacy policy

Graeme Mathew Digital · Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy explains how Graeme Mathew Digital (“I”, “me”) handles information. I am a sole trader based in Scotland, UK. The data controller is Graeme Mathew, trading as Graeme Mathew Digital. You can contact me at hello@graememathewdigital.co.uk.

This website

This website is a simple information page. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not collect personal data through forms. If you email me, I will use your details only as described in the next section.

Enquiries and your personal data

  • What I hold: your name, email address and anything you include in your message.
  • Why and lawful basis: I use it to reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, to provide a quote or services. My lawful basis is legitimate interests (responding to enquiries sent to me) or, where you ask for a quote or services, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • How long I keep it: if no work follows, I delete enquiry emails after 12 months. If we work together, I keep relevant records for as long as needed to deliver the work and to meet legal and accounting obligations.
  • Who sees it: I do not sell your data or use it for marketing. Emails sent to my business address are forwarded to, and stored in, my email account with Google.

Google user data

I use a private, self-hosted automation tool (n8n) to run my own workflows. These workflows connect to my own Google account through Google’s OAuth sign-in and may use the following Google services: Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Drive and Google Calendar.

  • What is accessed: only data in my own Google account, such as my emails, spreadsheets, files and calendar events, to run automations I have built for my own use.
  • How it is used: only to carry out those automations, for example sending myself reminders or updating my own spreadsheets.
  • Sharing: I do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone else, and I do not use it for advertising.
  • Storage: access credentials are stored on my own equipment. Data is processed there and is not kept for any other purpose.
  • No human review: no other person has access to this data.

My use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

I can revoke the app’s access to my Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask what personal data I hold about you (access);
  • ask me to correct it;
  • ask me to delete it;
  • ask me to restrict how I use it;
  • object to my use of it; and
  • ask for a copy of it in a portable format (portability).

To use any of these rights, email hello@graememathewdigital.co.uk. I will respond within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how I handle your personal data, please contact me first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If I change how I use information, I will update this page and the date above.
